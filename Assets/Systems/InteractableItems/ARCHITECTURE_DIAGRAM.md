## Detection Method Priority

```
????????????????????????????????????????????????????????
? DETECTION METHOD WATERFALL                           ?
????????????????????????????????????????????????????????
?                                                      ?
?  ?? RAYCAST (Primary - Most Reliable)               ?
?     • Physics2D.RaycastNonAlloc                     ?
?     • Checks parent & child colliders               ?
?     • Layer mask filtering                          ?
?     • Zero GC allocations                           ?
?                  ?                                   ?
?                  ?                                   ?
?  ?? TRIGGER COLLIDER (Interaction Zones)            ?
?     • Collider2D.OverlapPoint                       ?
?     • Prioritizes isTrigger = true                  ?
?     • For designated interaction areas              ?
?                  ?                                   ?
?                  ?                                   ?
?  ?? SOLID COLLIDER (Physics Shapes)                 ?
?     • Collider2D.OverlapPoint                       ?
?     • For objects without trigger zones             ?
?                  ?                                   ?
?                  ?                                   ?
?  ?? RADIUS (Fallback)                               ?
?     • Vector2.Distance < hoverCheckRadius           ?
?     • For objects without colliders                 ?
?     • Default: 1.0 unity units                      ?
?                  ?                                   ?
?                  ?                                   ?
?  ?? DIRECTIONAL (Stardew Valley Style)              ?
?     • Player?Object angle vs Player?Mouse angle     ?
?     • Only if < directionalHoverMaxDistance         ?
?     • Tolerance: 60° (configurable)                 ?
?     • Skipped for doors/teleports                   ?
```
